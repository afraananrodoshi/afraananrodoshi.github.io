I am a physician and a 2025 graduate of Mugda Medical College and Hospital, University of Dhaka, applying through ERAS for residency in the 2027 Match in Internal Medicine and Family Medicine.

My clinical training in Bangladesh covered the full MBBS curriculum and its rotations in medicine, surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, and paediatrics. Since January 2026 I have completed three clinical externships in the United States — in infectious disease, adult primary care, and gastroenterology — where I took histories, performed physical examinations, documented encounters in the EMR, and presented patients to supervising physicians.

Alongside clinical work I have published on the oral health burden of diabetes mellitus and on parathyroid function in transfusion-dependent thalassemia, and I presented a poster on endometrial cancer risk stratification in polycystic ovary syndrome at the 45th BMANA Convention. Community health has been a constant thread: six years of volunteering with Sandhani in blood donation, dengue awareness, and rural and urban outreach, and field research in rural communities during the COVID-19 period.
