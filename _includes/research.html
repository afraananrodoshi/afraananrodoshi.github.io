Diabetes Mellitus and Oral Health: A Review Article

Afra Anan Rodoshi
Pacific Journal of Medical Research First author

A narrative review with an observational analysis of 103 patients with type 2 diabetes, assessing the oral health burden of diabetes mellitus. Periodontal deterioration emerged as the strongest predictor of poor oral outcomes.

Parathyroid Hormone Status among Children with Transfusion-Dependent Thalassemia (TDT)

Mehnaz Tabassum, Md Mamun Newaz, Afra Anan Rodoshi, Papia Sultana, Hemamalini Roy, Anal Chandra Das, Md Sharif Ahsan, Md Jafar Islam, Md Abdur Rouf
Pacific Journal of Medical Research Co-author

A cross-sectional study of 103 children with transfusion-dependent thalassemia showing a significant negative correlation between iron overload and parathyroid function, with hypoparathyroidism identified in 18.4% of the cohort.
